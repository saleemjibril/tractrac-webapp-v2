ISSAM

Improving Smallholder Farmers' Access to Small-Scale Mechanisation Services

A strategic partnership between TracTrac Mechanisation Services Limited and the Mastercard Foundation, designed to improve how smallholder farmers in Nigeria access affordable and reliable mechanisation services.

Five-year objectives

A people-first model for mechanisation.

Youth-led Mechanisation Service Providers, a strong focus on women and persons with disabilities, technical skills, small-scale equipment and cooperative structures.

01

Create jobs

Empower cooperatives to deliver mechanisation services within their communities.

02

Bridge inclusion gaps

Actively involve women, youth and persons with disabilities in mechanisation opportunities.

03

Strengthen agricultural resilience

Combine technology, affordability and hands-on training to strengthen local mechanisation capacity.

The ISSAM journey

From community advocacy to equipment in the field.

  1. 01

    Advocacy

    Stakeholder engagement with traditional rulers, LGA chairpersons and district heads.

  2. 02

    Sensitisation

    Community awareness through religious houses, marketplaces and digital channels.

  3. 03

    Mobilisation

    25,000+ potential MSPs mobilised and screened across 2 states and 22 LGAs.

  4. 04

    Training

    18 cohorts across two years in agribusiness, leadership, machine use and maintenance.

  1. 05

    Work Enabling Outreach

    5,012 youths and women graduated as certified MSPs and received IT tools.

  2. 06

    Enterprises

    566 youth- and women-led cooperatives formed and elections conducted.

  3. 07

    Youth in Work

    2,987 MSPs currently earning.

  4. 08

    Equipment

    Labour-saving devices, tryctors and implements deployed.

Inclusion by design

Half the participants are women.

Participant profile: youth aged 18–35, 50% women and 50% men, 5% persons with disabilities.
