# Technology page — asset inventory (verified by inspection)

## Key finding
All the screenshots supplied are **TracTrac Plus**, not TracTrac Agent.
`Tractrac Plus.png` is the Plus home menu, and its tiles are:
ISSAM · Hire a Tractor · Become an Agent · Enlist your Tractor ·
Enlist as Operator/Mechanic · Invest in Tractors · Register as Vendors ·
Measure your Farm · Payment · Other Services.
Bottom nav: Home / Dashboard / Support / Profile.

So the enlistment, dashboard, verification and support screens all live
INSIDE Plus. Plus is not only farmer-facing — it is the single app for
farmers, equipment owners, agents and operators.

TracTrac Agent exists only as three screen recordings:
Booking Management · Track your tractor · Wallet. No stills supplied.
