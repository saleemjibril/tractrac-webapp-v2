Impact

What happens when access changes.

From farmers getting access to machinery, to young people building mechanisation businesses. The impact is in the people, machines, land and systems involved.

The work so far

More farmers served. More machines working. More opportunities created.

Better access to mechanisation can change how quickly work gets done, how many farmers a machine can serve and how many people can build livelihoods around the sector.

06Systems

Beyond the farm: policy and investment.

Through ISSAM, TracTrac supported the development of the National Agricultural Mechanisation Policy (NAMP) 2026 and the Mechanisation Investment Strategy Document. NAMP 2026 has been officially ratified by government.
