Partners

We do more when we build together.

TracTrac works with organisations across agriculture, finance, government and development to make mechanisation more accessible and sustainable.

Why we partner

Scaling access takes more than equipment.

It takes finance, skills, service networks, policy and long term investment.

TracTrac works with government, financial institutions, development partners, investors, equipment companies and agricultural organisations to strengthen the system around mechanisation.

What partnerships make possible

Partnerships, measured by the work they make happen.

01ISSAM × Mastercard Foundation

Building youth-led mechanisation services.

ISSAM is a strategic partnership between TracTrac Mechanisation Services Limited and the Mastercard Foundation.

The partnership supports TracTrac’s ISSAM programme to improve access to small-scale mechanisation for smallholder farmers while building the capacity of youth-led Mechanisation Service Providers.

WhereNasarawa and Kaduna states
WhoYouth aged 18–35; 50% women, 50% men; 5% persons with disabilities
ModelYouth-led MSPs, technical skills, small-scale equipment and cooperative structures
Explore ISSAM

02Equipment distribution

Getting equipment to farmer cooperatives.

In September 2025, the ISSAM equipment distribution delivered Tryctors, disc ploughs, harrows, planters and tractor buckets to farmer cooperatives in Nasarawa and Kaduna States.

WhenSeptember 2025
EquipmentTryctors, disc ploughs, harrows, planters and tractor buckets
WhereFarmer cooperatives in Nasarawa and Kaduna States
Implementation partnersThe ETK Group · Women in Mechanized Agriculture (WIMA) · Sane Consults

03Government & policy

Contributing to national mechanisation policy.

Through ISSAM, TracTrac engaged public institutions across agriculture, mechanisation, research, investment and policy implementation.

Who10+ key government institutions
Discussed2 high-level national policy dialogues; 8 investment promotion engagements
ProducedNAMP 2026, reviewed by 70+ stakeholders in 4 thematic groups and ratified at an event with 500+ delegates
TracTrac’s roleSupport to the development of NAMP 2026 and the Mechanisation Investment Strategy Document
