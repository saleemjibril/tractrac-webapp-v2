About TracTrac

We are building the systems that make mechanisation work.

TracTrac is a Nigerian agricultural mechanisation company. We connect smallholder farmers to affordable tractor services by bringing together equipment, technology, private investment and the people who deliver the work.

Why we exist

For too many farmers, the machine they need is too far away, too expensive or too hard to book.

TracTrac exists to change that.

We work to improve the lives of smallholder farmers by bringing private-sector investment, mechanisation and technology together, so affordable tractor services reach more farms across Africa. That means connecting five things:

Farmerswho need machines at the right time in the season
Machinestractors, implements and labour-saving devices
Technologyapps that match requests to equipment and track the work
Capitalfinancing that helps equipment reach the people who use it
Peopleoperators, agents and service providers who run the work

What we have built

A connected system for getting machines, people and capital to where the work happens.

01TracTrac PlusThe app farmers use to request tractors and mechanisation services.02TracTrac AgentThe tool booking agents use to take requests, manage bookings and track work in their communities.03MSP networksYouth-led Mechanisation Service Providers trained and equipped to deliver services locally.04Cooperative structuresMSPs organised into cooperatives that share equipment and run as businesses.05Equipment deploymentTractors, implements and labour-saving devices placed where farmers need them.06Financing pathwaysModels that help equipment owners and investors put machines to work.07ProgrammesStructured initiatives including ISSAM, Young People in Mechanisation and TRAxCelerate.

Recent milestones.

A selection of documented milestones from TracTrac’s programme and policy work.

2023ISSAM beginsTracTrac and the Mastercard Foundation start the ISSAM project, planned to run to 2028 across two phases.
September 2025Equipment to cooperativesISSAM distributes Tryctors, ploughs, harrows, planters and tractor buckets to farmer cooperatives in Nasarawa and Kaduna.
9 March 2026Inclusive mechanisation strategyTracTrac launches its Inclusive Agricultural Mechanization Strategy at a convening aligned with International Women’s Day.
August 2026National policy ratifiedTracTrac and the Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security convene the High-Level National Policy Dialogue, where NAMP 2026 is ratified.

How we work

Five principles behind the work.

02

03

People

Our vision

Leading a mechanisation revolution in Africa.

Our mission

To improve farmers’ access to mechanisation, create opportunities for investors and enterprising young people, increase the number of tractors available and use technology to make better use of every machine.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to.

Inclusion

Create opportunities for farmers regardless of size or resources.

Innovation

Develop practical technology that improves agricultural mechanisation.

Accountability

Take responsibility for commitments and maintain trust.

Sustainability

Support long-term agricultural productivity and responsible practices.

Collaboration

Work with farmers, investors and stakeholders to build the system together.

Respect

Value farmers and the people who make food production possible.
